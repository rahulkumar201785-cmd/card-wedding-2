OPEN ON YOUR COMPUTER
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the Jai_Manisha_Premium_Invitation folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open in Chrome or Edge.
4. Click Open Invitation; scroll to the scratch card and use your mouse/finger.

The invitation works locally. Internet is only needed for optional Google Fonts and map directions.

PUBLISH A SHAREABLE LINK
Upload index.html, style.css and script.js to a static host such as Netlify Drop or GitHub Pages, then share the public URL.
